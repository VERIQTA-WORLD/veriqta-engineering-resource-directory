# Cloud Observability

Find the signal that answers your workload question, then choose provider collection and query material.

## Follow the evidence

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Amazon CloudWatch](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudWatch/latest/monitoring/WhatIsCloudWatch.html) | Find the metric, log, or alarm reference behind a provider observation. | Free reference; telemetry charges may apply |
| [Azure Monitor](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-monitor/overview) | Choose the signal and collection workflow matching your Azure workload. | Free reference; telemetry charges may apply |
| [Google Cloud Observability](https://cloud.google.com/stackdriver/docs/overview) | Discover provider monitoring, logging, and related investigation references. | Free reference; usage charges may apply |
| [OpenTelemetry Demo](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/demo/) | Explore an instrumented application and its signals. | Free source; local or hosted infrastructure required |
| [Prometheus: Up & Running, Second Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/prometheus-up/9781098131135/) | Julien Pivotto and Brian Brazil; choose chapters on collection, queries, and alerting. | Paid book or subscription |
