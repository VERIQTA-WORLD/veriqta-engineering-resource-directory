# AWS: Learning, Workshops, and References

Start with one workload or service. Choose a structured lesson, a workshop, or a reference according to what you need to do next.

## Start learning

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AWS Skill Builder](https://skillbuilder.aws/) | Find AWS learning by service or role; inspect the selected course’s lab access. | Free and paid offerings; sign-in varies |
| [AWS Workshops](https://workshops.aws/) | Choose a workshop by service and inspect its setup, account, and teardown instructions. | Free instructions; AWS charges may apply |
| [AWS video channel](https://www.youtube.com/@amazonwebservices) | Browse the publisher’s videos and identify the service and date before following a demonstration. | Public video catalog; cloud and event access separate |
| [Amazon Builders’ Library](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/) | Explore engineering articles about operating distributed services. | Free engineering library |
| [AWS documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/) | Find the service reference matching your implementation question. | Free reference; service usage can incur charges |
| [AWS Architecture Center](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/) | Browse architecture material by workload and service concern. | Public architecture catalog |

## Choose a bounded exercise

Draw a small learning application with its identity, network, compute, and storage dependencies before creating resources. Check the selected guide’s permissions, region, account, and resource requirements before starting. Follow its teardown, revoke temporary access, and inspect retained disks, snapshots, addresses, logs, or other billable resources afterward.
