# Linux and Shell Challenges

Choose an exercise that makes you investigate files, processes, permissions, or service behavior.

## Choose your challenge

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OverTheWire Bandit](https://overthewire.org/wargames/bandit/) | Practise shell navigation and problem solving through progressive levels. | Free; SSH client required |
| [SadServers](https://sadservers.com/) | Investigate a broken system and explain the evidence behind your fix. | Free trials and paid plans |
| [The Missing Semester](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) | Lessons and exercises for shell workflows, version control, debugging, and other everyday tools. | Free course material |
| [The Linux Command Line](https://linuxcommand.org/tlcl.php) | William Shotts; build a working understanding of the shell, files, permissions, and scripts. | Free PDF; paid print editions |
| [ShellCheck](https://www.shellcheck.net/) | Examine a shell snippet and connect reported issues to the relevant explanation. | Free interactive tool |

## Make the exercise useful

Solve one level or broken-service scenario and explain each observation used in the investigation. Keep a short command-and-reasoning record with the sensitive output removed.

**Environment and cleanup:** Use the platform’s learning environment or a disposable VM; restore changed permissions and stop or remove the environment after practice.
