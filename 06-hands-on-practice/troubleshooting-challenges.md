# Troubleshooting Challenges

Find a scenario where you have to choose evidence before making a change.

## Choose an investigation

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SadServers](https://sadservers.com/) | Investigate a broken system and explain the evidence behind your fix. | Free trials and paid plans |
| [OverTheWire Bandit](https://overthewire.org/wargames/bandit/) | Practise shell navigation and problem solving through progressive levels. | Free; SSH client required |
| [SharkFest retrospective](https://sharkfest.wireshark.org/retrospective/) | Explore packet-analysis talks, slides, and specialist sessions. | Archive; recording access varies |
| [Brendan Gregg: Learning Linux performance](https://www.brendangregg.com/linuxperf.html) | Choose a performance method, visualization, or lecture around a question you can measure. | Free author material; linked books separate |
| [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) | Select a chapter about objectives, alerts, incidents, or releases rather than reading everything in order. | Free online book |

## Make the exercise useful

Write down the symptom, competing explanations, and the observation that changes your next step. Keep the evidence supporting the fix and a check that the original symptom is gone.

**Environment and cleanup:** Use owned or provided learning environments; undo test faults and preserve only redacted investigation notes.
