# Networking Labs and Captures

Practise a network question in a topology you can draw and control.

## Choose the kind of lab

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [containerlab](https://containerlab.dev/) | Build reproducible network labs from topology definitions. | Free documentation and source; compatible host and images required |
| [Cisco DevNet Sandbox](https://developer.cisco.com/site/sandbox/) | Inspect available networking labs and their reservation requirements. | Lab catalog; account and availability conditions |
| [Juniper vLabs](https://jlabs.juniper.net/vlabs/) | Explore vendor-provided network topologies and lab access. | Account, reservation, and availability conditions apply |
| [Subnetting practice](https://subnetipv4.com/) | Practise IPv4 subnet questions and check your reasoning. | Free learning website |
| [Networking Fundamentals](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/index/networking-fundamentals-how-data-moves-through-the-internet/) | Follow the free introductory module on how data moves through networks. | Free module; further training separate |
| [Stanford CS144](https://cs144.github.io/) | Study computer networking through a university course and programming assignments. | Public material; C++ and systems prerequisites |

## Make the exercise useful

Compare one permitted connection with a failed connection and identify the layer supported by your evidence. Keep the topology, endpoints, route or capture observations, and recovery check.

**Environment and cleanup:** Capture only authorized traffic; stop lab nodes, release reservations, and remove sensitive captures.
