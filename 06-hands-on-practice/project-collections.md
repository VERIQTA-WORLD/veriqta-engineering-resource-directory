## Make the exercise useful

Pick one application journey and extend it with a delivery check, investigation, or recovery exercise. Keep source configuration, prerequisites, verification, and a short explanation of a failure you resolved.

**Environment and cleanup:** Inventory created compute, storage, credentials, and hosted integrations, then remove what you no longer need.
