# Automation Exercises

Start with a small task you can explain manually, then automate only its understood behavior.

## Choose your language or workflow

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Exercism](https://exercism.org/) | Select a programming-language track and solve small exercises. | Free; account needed for platform features |
| [Automate the Boring Stuff with Python](https://automatetheboringstuff.com/) | Al Sweigart; choose a small automation task and study the relevant Python chapter. | Free online reading; paid editions |
| [Learn Go with Tests](https://quii.gitbook.io/learn-go-with-tests) | Chris James; learn Go by writing and examining tests. | Free online book |
| [The Missing Semester](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) | Lessons and exercises for shell workflows, version control, debugging, and other everyday tools. | Free course material |
| [Ansible 101 with Jeff Geerling](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2020/ansible-101-jeff-geerling-youtube-streaming-series/) | Watch the episode matching your task; the page provides topic markers and recordings. | Free video series; older examples |

## Make the exercise useful

Parse synthetic input or configure a learning service with a clear failure response. Keep the normal, empty, and malformed cases plus the result expected from each.

**Environment and cleanup:** Keep tokens out of fixtures, restrict effects to a learning scope, and remove test files or resources created by the automation.
