# Find Somewhere to Practise

Choose an environment or challenge that lets you check what you understand. Read the selected exercise’s setup and access conditions before you begin.

| Discover | What to explore | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Killercoda](https://killercoda.com/) | Browser environments and creator-built scenarios; inspect the prerequisites and goal of your chosen scenario | Free and paid options; session and account conditions apply |
| [SadServers](https://sadservers.com/) | Troubleshooting challenges on live systems; start with a scenario matching the Linux or service knowledge you already have | Free instant trials and paid plans |
| [The Missing Semester](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) | Exercises accompanying shell, version-control, debugging, and other engineering-workflow lessons | Free material; use your own suitable environment |
| [Wireshark capture practice](../04-tools-and-technologies/wireshark.md#try-it-yourself) | Inspect supplied traffic rather than creating a live capture first | Free source material; Wireshark installation needed |

For each exercise, identify the result you should verify and how to stop or remove its environment. A browser lab’s completion indicator is useful; explaining the observations is stronger evidence of understanding.

Further practice collections are being assembled. See the [practice file map](../FILE-MAP.md#06-hands-on-practice).
