# Certification Resources

Start with the issuing body’s current exam scope, then choose learning and practice for your actual knowledge gaps.

## Find the authoritative exam page

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AWS Certification](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/) | Use the issuer’s exam pages to confirm scope and preparation resources. | Exam fees and current terms apply |
| [Microsoft Credentials](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/) | Find the current Microsoft assessment and certification information. | Exam or assessment terms vary |
| [Google Cloud certification](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification) | Find the issuer’s exam requirements and preparation directions. | Exam fees and current terms apply |
| [Certified Kubernetes Administrator](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-kubernetes-administrator-cka/) | Read the issuer’s current scope, environment, and exam requirements. | Paid exam; current terms apply |
| [Linux Foundation training](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/) | Compare courses and certifications by scope and prerequisites. | Free and paid offerings |
| [Killercoda](https://killercoda.com/) | Choose a browser scenario or playground with a clear task and prerequisites. | Free and paid options; session limits |

## Before you choose

Check exam version, objectives, fees, identity requirements, remote-proctor rules, and retake terms on the issuer’s page. Use legitimate practice; avoid material marketed as leaked exam questions.
