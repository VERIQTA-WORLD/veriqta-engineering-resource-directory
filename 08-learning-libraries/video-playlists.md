# Video Series and Playlists

Use a sequence when you want the examples and explanations to build on one another.

## Follow a teaching sequence

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ansible 101 with Jeff Geerling](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2020/ansible-101-jeff-geerling-youtube-streaming-series/) | Watch the episode matching your task; the page provides topic markers and recordings. | Free video series; older examples |
| [Kubernetes 101 with Jeff Geerling](https://kube101.jeffgeerling.com/) | Follow an application through Kubernetes concepts with a creator’s recorded teaching series. | Free video series; check current APIs |
| [Networking Fundamentals](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/index/networking-fundamentals-how-data-moves-through-the-internet/) | Follow the free introductory module on how data moves through networks. | Free module; further training separate |
| [Practical TLS: free first module](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/practical-tls/practical-tls-free-ssl-training-m1/) | Use the introductory lessons before investigating certificate and TLS behavior. | Free module; full course separate |
| [Practical OSPF: free first module](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/stand-alone/ospf-training-course-free-m1/) | Study the routing-protocol introduction before an OSPF lab. | Free module; full course separate |
| [The Missing Semester](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) | Lessons and exercises for shell workflows, version control, debugging, and other everyday tools. | Free course material |
