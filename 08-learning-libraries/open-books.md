# Open Books and Public Textbooks

Choose a chapter for the concept you need rather than treating every book as a cover-to-cover assignment.

## Read without buying a full course

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Pro Git](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2) | Scott Chacon and Ben Straub; read the chapter matching the Git operation you need to understand. | Free online book |
| [The Linux Command Line](https://linuxcommand.org/tlcl.php) | William Shotts; build a working understanding of the shell, files, permissions, and scripts. | Free PDF; paid print editions |
| [Computer Networks: A Systems Approach](https://book.systemsapproach.org/) | Larry Peterson and Bruce Davie; connect protocols to the systems they support. | Free online textbook |
| [Beej’s Guide to Network Programming](https://beej.us/guide/bgnet/) | Brian Hall; work through socket programming when you need the application-side view of networking. | Free online book; C familiarity |
| [Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/) | Study operating-system concepts with free chapters and associated exercises. | Free online textbook |
| [Learn Go with Tests](https://quii.gitbook.io/learn-go-with-tests) | Chris James; learn Go by writing and examining tests. | Free online book |
| [Automate the Boring Stuff with Python](https://automatetheboringstuff.com/) | Al Sweigart; choose a small automation task and study the relevant Python chapter. | Free online reading; paid editions |
| [FreeBSD Handbook](https://docs.freebsd.org/en/books/handbook/) | Read the relevant storage, networking, service, or installation chapter alongside a FreeBSD lab. | Free online handbook |
| [everything curl](https://everything.curl.dev/) | Daniel Stenberg’s project book; use it to understand transfers and troubleshoot curl behavior. | Free online book |
| [Site Reliability Engineering](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) | Google’s SRE book; read for the operating responsibility behind the tools. | Free online book |
| [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) | Select a chapter about objectives, alerts, incidents, or releases rather than reading everything in order. | Free online book |

## Before you choose

Free-to-read material may still have copyright and redistribution conditions. Link to the author or publisher and check the edition before following examples.
