# Engineering Podcasts

Choose a conversation that develops a technical question you care about.

## Find a discussion to follow

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Changelog podcasts](https://changelog.com/podcasts) | Explore engineering and open-source conversations through their episode summaries and references. | Public podcast catalog; optional memberships separate |
| [Talk Python To Me](https://talkpython.fm/) | Choose an episode about the Python tool or practice you want to investigate. | Public podcast; associated courses separate |
| [Packet Pushers](https://packetpushers.net/) | Discover networking conversations and follow the episode’s technical references. | Public podcast catalog; access varies by offering |
| [Linux Unplugged](https://linuxunplugged.com/) | Explore Linux and self-hosting discussions with episode notes. | Public podcast; related memberships separate |

## Before you choose

Episode feeds are catalogs. Read an episode’s summary and follow the original references when a claim affects an implementation decision.
