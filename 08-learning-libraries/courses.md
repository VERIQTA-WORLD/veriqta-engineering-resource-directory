# Courses and Structured Learning

Compare a course’s prerequisites, syllabus, exercises, and access terms with the task you want to accomplish.

## Choose a course by outcome

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Missing Semester](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) | Lessons and exercises for shell workflows, version control, debugging, and other everyday tools. | Free course material |
| [AWS Skill Builder](https://skillbuilder.aws/) | Find AWS learning by service or role; inspect the selected course’s lab access. | Free and paid offerings; sign-in varies |
| [Azure training](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/azure/) | Select a module matching the Azure service you are using. | Free reading; exercise accounts and charges vary |
| [Google Cloud training](https://cloud.google.com/learn/training) | Choose a learning path, course, or lab for your current provider task. | Mixed access; account and lab terms vary |
| [Linux Foundation training](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/) | Compare courses and certifications by scope and prerequisites. | Free and paid offerings |
| [Web Security Academy](https://portswigger.net/web-security) | Choose a vulnerability explanation and its authorized training lab. | Free learning and labs; account for progress |
| [Ansible 101 with Jeff Geerling](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2020/ansible-101-jeff-geerling-youtube-streaming-series/) | Watch the episode matching your task; the page provides topic markers and recordings. | Free video series; older examples |
| [Kubernetes 101 with Jeff Geerling](https://kube101.jeffgeerling.com/) | Follow an application through Kubernetes concepts with a creator’s recorded teaching series. | Free video series; check current APIs |
| [Networking Fundamentals](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/index/networking-fundamentals-how-data-moves-through-the-internet/) | Follow the free introductory module on how data moves through networks. | Free module; further training separate |

## Before you choose

Separate course access from labs, subscriptions, exam vouchers, and infrastructure charges. A completion certificate does not replace evidence that you can apply the material.
