# Engineering Newsletters

Choose a publication you can use to discover resources, not another feed you feel obliged to finish.

## Find a useful recurring selection

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SRE Weekly](https://sreweekly.com/) | Discover reliability articles and follow the original accounts. | Public newsletter archive |
| [KubeWeekly](https://www.cncf.io/kubeweekly/) | Find a recurring selection of cloud-native updates and resources. | Public newsletter; subscription optional |
| [Last Week in AWS](https://www.lastweekinaws.com/) | Discover AWS commentary and linked material with attention to the author’s perspective. | Public publication; subscription terms apply |
| [CNCF Blog](https://www.cncf.io/blog/) | Follow community and project updates to their original material. | Public publication |

## Before you choose

Compare the linked original material before acting on a summary. Read the publisher’s subscription and privacy terms before providing your email.
