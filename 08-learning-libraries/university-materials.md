# University Course Material

Use public lectures, readings, and assignments when you want a more rigorous foundation.

## Choose the prerequisite depth

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Missing Semester](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) | Lessons and exercises for shell workflows, version control, debugging, and other everyday tools. | Free course material |
| [MIT distributed systems](https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/6.824/) | Explore lectures, readings, and labs for distributed-system reasoning. | Public course material; substantial programming prerequisites |
| [Stanford CS144](https://cs144.github.io/) | Study computer networking through a university course and programming assignments. | Public material; C++ and systems prerequisites |
| [Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/) | Study operating-system concepts with free chapters and associated exercises. | Free online textbook |
| [Computer Networks: A Systems Approach](https://book.systemsapproach.org/) | Larry Peterson and Bruce Davie; connect protocols to the systems they support. | Free online textbook |

## Before you choose

Public material does not imply enrollment, instructor support, assessment, or academic credit. Check programming and mathematics prerequisites before starting a lab.
