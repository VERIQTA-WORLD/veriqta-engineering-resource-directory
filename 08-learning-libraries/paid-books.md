# Books Worth Comparing

Choose a book for a specific gap, inspect its contents and sample, and check the edition before purchasing.

## Compare the subject and level

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Learn Docker in a Month of Lunches](https://www.manning.com/books/learn-docker-in-a-month-of-lunches) | Elton Stoneman; follow a progressive application-oriented route through container use. | Paid book; publisher samples |
| [Kubernetes in Action, Second Edition](https://www.manning.com/books/kubernetes-in-action-second-edition) | Marko Lukša and Kevin Conner; use the contents to select the workload or cluster subject you need. | Paid book; publisher samples |
| [Terraform: Up & Running](https://www.terraformupandrunning.com/) | Yevgeniy Brikman; connect infrastructure definitions with state, collaboration, and change management. | Paid book; author resources |
| [Ansible for DevOps](https://www.ansiblefordevops.com/) | Jeff Geerling; follow configuration-management examples and compare them with your own host environment. | Paid book; public companion material |
| [Prometheus: Up & Running, Second Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/prometheus-up/9781098131135/) | Julien Pivotto and Brian Brazil; choose chapters on collection, queries, and alerting. | Paid book or subscription |
| [Systems Performance](https://www.brendangregg.com/systems-performance-2nd-edition-book.html) | Brendan Gregg; study how to frame and investigate performance questions across a system. | Paid book; author material |
| [BPF Performance Tools](https://www.brendangregg.com/bpf-performance-tools-book.html) | Brendan Gregg; explore tracing tools when ordinary counters cannot explain a Linux performance question. | Paid book; Linux familiarity needed |
| [Practical Packet Analysis, Third Edition](https://nostarch.com/packetanalysis3) | Chris Sanders; pair protocol explanations with companion captures. Interface examples use Wireshark 2.x. | Paid book; free sample and captures |
| [The Art of PostgreSQL](https://theartofpostgresql.com/) | Dimitri Fontaine; deepen SQL reasoning with a PostgreSQL-focused book. | Paid book; author samples |
| [High Performance MySQL, Fourth Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/high-performance-mysql/9781492080503/) | Silvia Botros and Jeremy Tinley; study operating and performance trade-offs. | Paid book or subscription |
| [RabbitMQ in Depth](https://www.manning.com/books/rabbitmq-in-depth) | Gavin M. Roy; study messaging patterns beyond a first publish-and-consume example. | Paid book; publisher samples |
| [Kafka: The Definitive Guide, Second Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/kafka-the-definitive/9781492043072/) | Gwen Shapira and coauthors; examine Kafka concepts and operating concerns. | Paid book or subscription |
| [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://dataintensive.net/) | Martin Kleppmann’s author site; compare editions and select the data-system question you need to study. | Paid book; author resources |
| [Learn PowerShell in a Month of Lunches, Fourth Edition](https://www.manning.com/books/learn-powershell-in-a-month-of-lunches) | A structured PowerShell learning route; follow the edition’s environment requirements. | Paid book; publisher samples |

## Before you choose

Publisher prices, formats, and regional availability can change. A paid book is optional; use the topic and tool pages to find free alternatives and references.
