# Engineering YouTube Channels

Choose a teacher or publisher by the work you want to understand, then open a specific series rather than browsing indefinitely.

## Find a channel worth exploring

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Learn Linux TV](https://www.learnlinux.tv/) | Discover Linux demonstrations and topic series from Jay LaCroix. | Public videos and articles; separate paid products |
| [TechWorld with Nana](https://www.techworld-with-nana.com/) | Discover the creator’s teaching and public video channel; distinguish free videos from paid programs. | Creator catalog; mixed access |
| [Ansible 101 with Jeff Geerling](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2020/ansible-101-jeff-geerling-youtube-streaming-series/) | Watch the episode matching your task; the page provides topic markers and recordings. | Free video series; older examples |
| [Networking Fundamentals](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/index/networking-fundamentals-how-data-moves-through-the-internet/) | Follow the free introductory module on how data moves through networks. | Free module; further training separate |
| [SharkFest retrospective](https://sharkfest.wireshark.org/retrospective/) | Explore packet-analysis talks, slides, and specialist sessions. | Archive; recording access varies |
| [CNCF online programs](https://www.cncf.io/online-programs/) | Browse project and practitioner sessions; choose a recording about your current cloud-native question. | Event catalog; session access varies |
| [AWS video channel](https://www.youtube.com/@amazonwebservices) | Browse the publisher’s videos and identify the service and date before following a demonstration. | Public video catalog; cloud and event access separate |
| [Google Cloud Tech](https://www.youtube.com/@GoogleCloudTech) | Explore the publisher’s demonstrations and technical series. | Public video catalog; cloud and training access separate |
| [Microsoft Learn Shows](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/shows/) | Browse Microsoft’s technical video series and select the relevant product or topic. | Free video catalog |

## Before you choose

A channel is a catalog, not a promise that every upload fits your level. Check the date, environment, and prerequisites of the individual video.
