# Contributing

Add resources to the most relevant collection. Use the [resource entry template](10-contributing-and-maintenance/resource-entry-template.md) and [review checklist](10-contributing-and-maintenance/resource-review-checklist.md). Describe the proposed improvement and how you verified it in your pull request.

Respect source authors, disclose access requirements and avoid duplicate descriptions. Do not submit credentials, private incident material, leaked exam content or unauthorised copies of paid resources.
