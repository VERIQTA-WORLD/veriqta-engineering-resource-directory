# Suggest a Discovery

Help someone find a resource worth opening. You can suggest a specific video, teaching website, book, article, capture collection, lab, conference session, or learning platform.

In an issue or pull request, include:

- The direct link and the resource title.
- The creator or publisher.
- The subject or tool it belongs with.
- One specific reason it helps.
- Free, paid, account, and prerequisite information that affects access.
- A useful starting lesson, chapter, exercise, or timestamp when you know one.

Prefer an original publisher link. Suggest legally available material and attribute its creator. Tell us when a recommendation involves your own work, a sponsorship, or another relevant relationship.

For edits, follow the [writing and curation standard](WRITING-STANDARD.md). Keep files directly inside the numbered folders. Keep a tool’s selected resources together rather than duplicating them across career pages.

To report an outdated entry, identify its file and explain what changed. A correction to access conditions or a more precise starting link is useful too.
