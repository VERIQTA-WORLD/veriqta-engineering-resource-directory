## Adjacent resource collections

| Career | Where the work overlaps | Resource emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [DevOps engineer](../devops-engineer/README.md) | Builds and operates delivery workflows and infrastructure automation. | Pipeline manuals, infrastructure tooling, deployment practices. |
| [DevOps automation engineer](../devops-automation-engineer/README.md) | Focuses on repeatable automation, integrations, scripts, and workflow failure handling. | Automation APIs, configuration guides, tests, and practical repositories. |
| [Platform architect](../../03-platform-engineering/platform-architect/README.md) | Designs platform boundaries, reusable capabilities, tenancy, and operating models. | Platform references, developer portals, identity, policy, and cost allocation. |
| [Platform engineer](../../03-platform-engineering/platform-engineer/README.md) | Implements and operates shared platform capabilities. | Kubernetes tooling, GitOps, templates, observability, and recovery. |
| [Site reliability engineer](../../02-sre-reliability/site-reliability-engineer/README.md) | Connects service objectives, operational work, incident response, and reliability improvements. | SRE books, alerting, failure containment, and incident resources. |
| [Cloud architect](../../04-cloud-engineering/cloud-architect/README.md) | Designs cloud workload and foundation choices within business and operational constraints. | Provider architectures, landing zones, networking, IAM, and disaster recovery. |
| [CI/CD engineer](../../10-cicd-release/cicd-engineer/README.md) | Implements pipeline execution, integrations, and delivery controls. | Runner documentation, pipeline tools, artifact flows, and credential security. |
| [Release engineer](../../10-cicd-release/release-engineer/README.md) | Coordinates release mechanics, packaging, versioning, promotion, and recovery. | Artifact repositories, progressive delivery, rollback compatibility, and provenance. |
| [DevSecOps engineer](../../12-security-devsecops/devsecops-engineer/README.md) | Integrates security practices and evidence into delivery and operations. | Security guidance, policy tools, supply-chain controls, and verification standards. |
| [Supply-chain security engineer](../../12-security-devsecops/supply-chain-security-engineer/README.md) | Focuses on dependency, build, artifact, provenance, and verification trust. | SBOM tools, signing, SLSA, and secure-development references. |
| [FinOps engineer](../../20-governance-finops/finops-engineer/README.md) | Connects technology usage and costs with allocation, accountability, and business decisions. | FinOps guidance, cloud review frameworks, estimates, and Kubernetes allocation tooling. |
| [Policy-as-code engineer](../../20-governance-finops/policy-as-code-engineer/README.md) | Builds, tests, and operates machine-evaluated policy controls. | Policy engines, admission behavior, test practices, and exception handling. |
