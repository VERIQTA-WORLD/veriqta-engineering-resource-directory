# DevOps Platform Engineer: Learning Resources

Start with the consumer task before studying the portal. Then separate metadata, execution, and authority so a convincing interface does not conceal an unsupported operation.

## Start with a workflow problem

Choose one user group and one recurring task. Document the current path and the evidence of friction. Read platform guidance to frame the capability, then use a small prototype to test whether the proposed interface actually helps.

## Study metadata and authority separately

Learn catalog ownership and template inputs alongside authentication and permission design. This prevents an early prototype from confusing discoverability with enforcement. Use synthetic organizations, repositories, and identities for experiments.

## Learn to operate what you build

Define acceptance, support, upgrade, and retirement paths before broad adoption. Ask a second person to complete the workflow using your documentation. Their questions expose assumptions that the author of a template may not notice.

## A focused reading list

- [CNCF Platforms White Paper](https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/whitepapers/platforms/): Platform capabilities and the platform-as-product discussion. [Access and technical limits](../README.md#source-platforms).
- [Backstage Software Catalog](https://backstage.io/docs/features/software-catalog/): Entity concepts and ownership information. [Access and technical limits](../README.md#source-catalog).
- [Backstage Software Templates](https://backstage.io/docs/features/software-templates/): Template structure and available actions. [Access and technical limits](../README.md#source-templates).
- [Backstage permissions overview](https://backstage.io/docs/permissions/overview/): Permission concepts and policy integration. [Access and technical limits](../README.md#source-permissions).
- [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/): The chapter corresponding to your current service concern. [Access and technical limits](../README.md#source-sre).

## Your next study decision

Design a learning project around one onboarding task. Include a consumer test, a denied request, an interrupted external action, and a documented support response before adding more integrations.
