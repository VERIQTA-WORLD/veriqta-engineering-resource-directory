# DevOps Consultant: Related Career Paths

## [DevOps Architect](../devops-architect/README.md)

Develop requirements, alternatives, and a transition design. Choose this route when discovery establishes a cross-system design problem.

Continue with [responsibilities](../devops-architect/devops-architect-role-and-responsibilities.md) or [architecture resources](../devops-architect/devops-architect-architecture-resources.md).

## [DevOps Engineer](../devops-engineer/README.md)

Implement and observe the selected service delivery change. Choose this route when the engagement moves from assessment to hands-on workflow work.

Continue with [responsibilities](../devops-engineer/devops-engineer-role-and-responsibilities.md) or [architecture resources](../devops-engineer/devops-engineer-architecture-resources.md).

## [DevOps Automation Engineer](../devops-automation-engineer/README.md)

Build a bounded repeated operation with failure semantics. Choose this route when the pilot depends on repeatability and diagnosability.

Continue with [responsibilities](../devops-automation-engineer/devops-automation-engineer-role-and-responsibilities.md) or [architecture resources](../devops-automation-engineer/devops-automation-engineer-architecture-resources.md).

## [DevOps Infrastructure Engineer](../devops-infrastructure-engineer/README.md)

Validate state, access, and environment lifecycle constraints. Choose this route when environment inconsistency blocks the proposed improvement.

Continue with [responsibilities](../devops-infrastructure-engineer/devops-infrastructure-engineer-role-and-responsibilities.md) or [architecture resources](../devops-infrastructure-engineer/devops-infrastructure-engineer-architecture-resources.md).

## [DevOps Platform Engineer](../devops-platform-engineer/README.md)

Develop a supported capability and adoption evidence. Choose this route when the client needs a shared service and has an operating owner.
