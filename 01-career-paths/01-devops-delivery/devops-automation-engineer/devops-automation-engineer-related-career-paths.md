# DevOps Automation Engineer: Related Career Paths

## [DevOps Engineer](../devops-engineer/README.md)

Connect a workflow to application and release outcomes. Choose this route when execution succeeds but service acceptance remains unclear.

Continue with [responsibilities](../devops-engineer/devops-engineer-role-and-responsibilities.md) or [architecture resources](../devops-engineer/devops-engineer-architecture-resources.md).

## [DevOps Architect](../devops-architect/README.md)

Examine system boundaries and cross-team control decisions. Choose this route when operation semantics depend on a larger delivery architecture.

Continue with [responsibilities](../devops-architect/devops-architect-role-and-responsibilities.md) or [architecture resources](../devops-architect/devops-architect-architecture-resources.md).

## [DevOps Infrastructure Engineer](../devops-infrastructure-engineer/README.md)

Investigate provider state and persistent resource ownership. Choose this route when retries or cleanup interact with infrastructure lifecycle.

Continue with [responsibilities](../devops-infrastructure-engineer/devops-infrastructure-engineer-role-and-responsibilities.md) or [architecture resources](../devops-infrastructure-engineer/devops-infrastructure-engineer-architecture-resources.md).

## [DevOps Platform Engineer](../devops-platform-engineer/README.md)

Expose a reusable operation through a consumer contract. Choose this route when teams need supported access, status, and recovery.

Continue with [responsibilities](../devops-platform-engineer/devops-platform-engineer-role-and-responsibilities.md) or [architecture resources](../devops-platform-engineer/devops-platform-engineer-architecture-resources.md).

## [DevOps Consultant](../devops-consultant/README.md)

Validate whether automating a task addresses a demonstrated constraint. Choose this route when the priority or expected benefit of automation is uncertain.

Continue with [responsibilities](../devops-consultant/devops-consultant-role-and-responsibilities.md) or [architecture resources](../devops-consultant/devops-consultant-architecture-resources.md).
