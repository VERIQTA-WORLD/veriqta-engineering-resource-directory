# Scalability Engineer: Related Career Paths

Which limit constrains useful throughput next, and will the proposed scaling action actually relieve it? When the next observation points to a different responsibility, use the routes below.

## [Capacity Engineer](../capacity-engineer/README.md)

Scaling analysis explains how a limit changes; capacity planning decides when usable resources must be ready. Use this route for forecast ranges, lead times, peak demand, and failure reserve.

Open [architecture resources](../capacity-engineer/capacity-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../capacity-engineer/capacity-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.

## [Database Reliability Engineer](../database-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on protecting database correctness, availability, performance, and recoverability as connected responsibilities. Ask: **Can you preserve correct data and useful service behavior through overload, replication failure, and restoration?**

Open [architecture resources](../database-reliability-engineer/database-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../database-reliability-engineer/database-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.

## [Platform Reliability Engineer](../platform-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on operating shared developer capabilities with explicit tenant boundaries, consumer objectives, and recoverable workflows. Ask: **Can consumers complete supported tasks when a shared controller, integration, or identity service is degraded?**

Open [architecture resources](../platform-reliability-engineer/platform-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../platform-reliability-engineer/platform-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.
