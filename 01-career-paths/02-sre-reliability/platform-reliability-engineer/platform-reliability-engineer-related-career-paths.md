# Platform Reliability Engineer: Related Career Paths

Can consumers complete supported tasks when a shared controller, integration, or identity service is degraded? When the next observation points to a different responsibility, use the routes below.

## [Infrastructure Reliability Engineer](../infrastructure-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on keeping infrastructure and its change-control dependencies operable, recoverable, and appropriately isolated. Ask: **Can you repair or rebuild the environment when the infrastructure control path itself is impaired?**

Open [architecture resources](../infrastructure-reliability-engineer/infrastructure-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../infrastructure-reliability-engineer/infrastructure-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.

## [Service Reliability Engineer](../service-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on connecting service ownership, user journeys, objectives, dependencies, releases, and recovery. Ask: **Which user outcome defines service health, and what evidence supports accepting or stopping a change?**

Open [architecture resources](../service-reliability-engineer/service-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../service-reliability-engineer/service-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.

## [Site Reliability Engineer](../site-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on applying software engineering to service reliability, operational workload, release safety, and incident learning. Ask: **Which engineering change will sustainably improve service reliability or reduce recurring operational work?**

Open [architecture resources](../site-reliability-engineer/site-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../site-reliability-engineer/site-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.
