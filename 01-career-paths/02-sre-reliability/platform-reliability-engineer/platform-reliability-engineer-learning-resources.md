# Platform Reliability Engineer: Learning Resources

Define useful consumer completion before choosing indicators. Trace permission and shared-workflow behavior next; these explain failures a portal availability check cannot see.

## Define the platform capability

Read [CNCF Platforms White Paper](https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/whitepapers/platforms/), beginning with platform capabilities and the platform-as-product discussion.

Write one consumer task, the platform contract, consumer responsibilities, and an acceptance condition.

## Trace permission enforcement

Read [Backstage permissions overview](https://backstage.io/docs/permissions/overview/), beginning with permission concepts and policy integration.

Map requester identity, decision, executor authority, and downstream action for two tenants.

## Understand contract compatibility

Read [GitHub Actions: Reuse workflows](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/reuse-automations/reuse-workflows), beginning with inputs, secrets, permissions, and version references.

List inputs, outputs, permissions, version references, and consumer migration requirements.

## Measure useful task completion

Read [Implementing SLOs](https://sre.google/workbook/implementing-slos/), beginning with selecting indicators, targets, and the improvement process.

Define accepted, queued, failed, partial, and completed requests with a meaningful measurement boundary.

Choose a [project](platform-reliability-engineer-labs-and-portfolio-projects.md) when you need execution evidence, or work through the [assessment scenarios](platform-reliability-engineer-interview-and-assessment-resources.md) to apply the idea under changed conditions.
