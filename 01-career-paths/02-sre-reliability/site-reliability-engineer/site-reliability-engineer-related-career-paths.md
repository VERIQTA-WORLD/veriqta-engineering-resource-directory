# Site Reliability Engineer: Related Career Paths

Which engineering change will sustainably improve service reliability or reduce recurring operational work? When the next observation points to a different responsibility, use the routes below.

## [Service Reliability Engineer](../service-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on connecting service ownership, user journeys, objectives, dependencies, releases, and recovery. Ask: **Which user outcome defines service health, and what evidence supports accepting or stopping a change?**

Open [architecture resources](../service-reliability-engineer/service-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../service-reliability-engineer/service-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.

## [Platform Reliability Engineer](../platform-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on operating shared developer capabilities with explicit tenant boundaries, consumer objectives, and recoverable workflows. Ask: **Can consumers complete supported tasks when a shared controller, integration, or identity service is degraded?**

Open [architecture resources](../platform-reliability-engineer/platform-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../platform-reliability-engineer/platform-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.

## [Reliability Engineer](../reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on turning user and business requirements into measurable reliability decisions across a system lifecycle. Ask: **Which failure risk matters most to users, and which intervention has evidence of reducing it?**

Open [architecture resources](../reliability-engineer/reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../reliability-engineer/reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.
