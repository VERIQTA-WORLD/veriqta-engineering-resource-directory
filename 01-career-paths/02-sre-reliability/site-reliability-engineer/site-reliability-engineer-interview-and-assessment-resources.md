# Site Reliability Engineer: Interview and Assessment Resources

These prompts ask you to connect incident evidence, operational workload, and an improvement the team can maintain.

## Restarts repeatedly clear the page

Explain how you would preserve evidence, distinguish mitigation from correction, and prioritize durable work. Compare memory growth, dependency exhaustion, queue behavior, and operational assumptions without prematurely selecting a cause.

## Reliability work always loses to features

Describe the evidence and agreed decision process you would use: user impact, objective consumption, incident cost, recurrence, and expected improvement. Avoid presenting an error budget as an automatic rule independent of stakeholder policy.

## What to look for in your answer

Connect mitigation to durable engineering work. Include user impact, recurring effort, maintenance, ownership, and the observation that would establish an improvement.
