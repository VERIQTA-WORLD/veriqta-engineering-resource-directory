# Service Reliability Engineer: Related Career Paths

Which user outcome defines service health, and what evidence supports accepting or stopping a change? When the next observation points to a different responsibility, use the routes below.

## [Site Reliability Engineer](../site-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on applying software engineering to service reliability, operational workload, release safety, and incident learning. Ask: **Which engineering change will sustainably improve service reliability or reduce recurring operational work?**

Open [architecture resources](../site-reliability-engineer/site-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../site-reliability-engineer/site-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.

## [Network Reliability Engineer](../network-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on maintaining dependable connectivity through naming, routing, transport, policy, and failure-domain analysis. Ask: **At which boundary does the connection fail, and which observation distinguishes the competing causes?**

Open [architecture resources](../network-reliability-engineer/network-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../network-reliability-engineer/network-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.

## [Database Reliability Engineer](../database-reliability-engineer/README.md)

This route focuses on protecting database correctness, availability, performance, and recoverability as connected responsibilities. Ask: **Can you preserve correct data and useful service behavior through overload, replication failure, and restoration?**

Open [architecture resources](../database-reliability-engineer/database-reliability-engineer-architecture-resources.md) for the design boundary, or [production practices](../database-reliability-engineer/database-reliability-engineer-production-practices.md) when you have an operating observation to investigate.
