# Reliability Engineer: Skills and Prerequisite Resources

The measurement boundary matters as much as the target: missing events and hidden user groups can distort a reassuring result.

## Learn measurement boundaries

Understand indicators, objectives, request classes, time windows, and data quality. Separate missing observations from successful work. Compare overall service results with the experience of important regions or users so aggregation does not hide a material failure.

## Develop failure and recovery reasoning

Distinguish trigger, contributing condition, propagation, detection, mitigation, and restoration. Evaluate data consequences and dependency availability. A timeline can reveal mechanisms without establishing a single exclusive cause.
