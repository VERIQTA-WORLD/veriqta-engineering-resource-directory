## Try a small task

Inspect an owned virtualization host and identify the components used by a learning guest.

**Keep:** The host capability and the guest-management path, without changing production settings.

**Setup:** owned host, sufficient capacity, and authorized guest images. **Cleanup:** disposable guests and their dedicated disks, snapshots, and networks.
