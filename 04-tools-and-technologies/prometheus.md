## Try a small task

Scrape a disposable target, stop it, and distinguish missing collection from a healthy response.

**Keep:** The target status and queries used to explain the change.

**Setup:** owned learning target; hosted telemetry can cost money. **Cleanup:** collectors, test services, retained data, and hosted resources.
