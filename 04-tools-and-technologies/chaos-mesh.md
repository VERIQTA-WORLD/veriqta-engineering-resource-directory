## Try a small task

Run one reversible experiment against a disposable application and verify recovery.

**Keep:** The hypothesis, affected scope, and before-and-after service evidence.

**Setup:** owned target, bounded load or fault, explicit stop condition. **Cleanup:** stop generators, undo faults, and verify recovery.
