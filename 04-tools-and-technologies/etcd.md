## Try a small task

Write a synthetic key, observe a change through a documented client, and remove it.

**Keep:** The client operations and the observed update.

**Setup:** dedicated synthetic dataset and suitable storage. **Cleanup:** test databases, queues, volumes, and accounts after preserving your results.
