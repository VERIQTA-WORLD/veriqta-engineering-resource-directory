## Try a small task

Inspect a small service in a disposable VM and identify its process, user, files, and listener.

**Keep:** A short dependency inventory and a verified service request.

**Setup:** synthetic files or a disposable host for administrative changes. **Cleanup:** altered settings, test accounts, keys, and learning VMs.
