## Try a small task

Create a VM, provision a small service, and recreate it from the definition.

**Keep:** The Vagrantfile and evidence that the recreated service behaves as intended.

**Setup:** dedicated learning scope; cloud examples can incur charges. **Cleanup:** test resources, retained images or storage, and temporary access.
