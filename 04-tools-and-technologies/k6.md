## Try a small task

Run a small bounded load against an owned test service and explain one threshold.

**Keep:** The script, load limits, and the observations behind the threshold result.

**Setup:** owned target, bounded load or fault, explicit stop condition. **Cleanup:** stop generators, undo faults, and verify recovery.
