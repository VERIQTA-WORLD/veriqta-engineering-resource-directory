# Linux: Shells, Processes, and Services

Choose a resource for the host behavior you want to explain.

## Start at the shell

**[The Linux Command Line →](https://linuxcommand.org/tlcl.php)**  
William Shotts; build a working understanding of the shell, files, permissions, and scripts.  
**Free PDF; paid print editions**

**[BashGuide →](https://mywiki.wooledge.org/BashGuide)**  
Study shell behavior through a community-maintained guide.  
**Free guide**

**[The Missing Semester →](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/)**  
Lessons and exercises for shell workflows, version control, debugging, and other everyday tools.  
**Free course material**

**[Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces →](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/)**  
Study operating-system concepts with free chapters and associated exercises.  
**Free online textbook**

**[Learn Linux TV →](https://www.learnlinux.tv/)**  
Discover Linux demonstrations and topic series from Jay LaCroix.  
**Public videos and articles; separate paid products**

**[OverTheWire Bandit →](https://overthewire.org/wargames/bandit/)**  
Practise shell navigation and problem solving through progressive levels.  
**Free; SSH client required**

**[SadServers →](https://sadservers.com/)**  
Investigate a broken system and explain the evidence behind your fix.  
**Free trials and paid plans**
