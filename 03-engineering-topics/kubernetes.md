# Kubernetes Workloads and Clusters

Choose a learning route for the workload, networking, or control-plane question behind your task.

## Learn the workload model

**[Kubernetes in Action, Second Edition →](https://www.manning.com/books/kubernetes-in-action-second-edition)**  
Marko Lukša and Kevin Conner; use the contents to select the workload or cluster subject you need.  
**Paid book; publisher samples**

**[Kubernetes 101 with Jeff Geerling →](https://kube101.jeffgeerling.com/)**  
Follow an application through Kubernetes concepts with a creator’s recorded teaching series.  
**Free video series; check current APIs**

**[Kube by Example →](https://kubebyexample.com/)**  
Choose a focused Kubernetes learning path or exercise.  
**Public learning material; environment requirements vary**

**[Killercoda →](https://killercoda.com/)**  
Choose a browser scenario or playground with a clear task and prerequisites.  
**Free and paid options; session limits**

**[Google Cloud microservices demo →](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/microservices-demo)**  
Read and deploy a sample application when you need a multi-service workload for a lab.  
**Free source; infrastructure charges may apply**
