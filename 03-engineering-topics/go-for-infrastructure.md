# Go for Infrastructure

Build confidence with short tested programs before writing a service or controller.

## Start with executable examples

**[Learn Go with Tests →](https://quii.gitbook.io/learn-go-with-tests)**  
Chris James; learn Go by writing and examining tests.  
**Free online book**

**[Go by Example →](https://gobyexample.com/)**  
Choose a small executable example for a specific Go concept.  
**Free examples**

**[Exercism →](https://exercism.org/)**  
Select a programming-language track and solve small exercises.  
**Free; account needed for platform features**

**[Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces →](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/)**  
Study operating-system concepts with free chapters and associated exercises.  
**Free online textbook**

## Put one idea to work

Write a small HTTP client and test success, timeout, and invalid-response behavior against an owned test service. Use the selected guide’s prerequisites and verification steps. Choose an owned or provided learning environment, keep sensitive data out of your notes, and remove the exercise resources when finished.
