# Platform Design

Choose material around a recurring developer task and the support needed to make it usable.

## Begin with the consumer

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CNCF Platforms White Paper](https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/whitepapers/platforms/) | Explore platform capabilities and the needs of the engineers who consume them. | Free white paper |
| [CNCF landscape](https://landscape.cncf.io/) | Discover cloud-native projects and follow their own learning material. | Free discovery map; not a ranking |
| [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) | Select a chapter about objectives, alerts, incidents, or releases rather than reading everything in order. | Free online book |

## Put one idea to work

Prototype one onboarding task, document its inputs and failure behavior, and ask someone else to try it. Use the selected guide’s prerequisites and verification steps. Choose an owned or provided learning environment, keep sensitive data out of your notes, and remove the exercise resources when finished.
