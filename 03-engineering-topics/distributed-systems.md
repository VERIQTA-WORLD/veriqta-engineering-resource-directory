# Distributed Systems

Study failure and coordination with resources that expose their assumptions.

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MIT distributed systems](https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/6.824/) | Explore lectures, readings, and labs for distributed-system reasoning. | Public course material; substantial programming prerequisites |
| [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://dataintensive.net/) | Martin Kleppmann’s author site; compare editions and select the data-system question you need to study. | Paid book; author resources |
| [Amazon Builders’ Library](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/) | Explore engineering articles about operating distributed services. | Free engineering library |
| [Site Reliability Engineering](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) | Google’s SRE book; read for the operating responsibility behind the tools. | Free online book |
| [Computer Networks: A Systems Approach](https://book.systemsapproach.org/) | Larry Peterson and Bruce Davie; connect protocols to the systems they support. | Free online textbook |
