# Databases and Querying

Choose SQL practice, data-modeling material, or operating references according to the problem you need to solve.

## Work with a known dataset

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SQLBolt](https://sqlbolt.com/) | Practise SQL queries in short browser lessons. | Free interactive exercises |
| [PostgreSQL Exercises](https://pgexercises.com/) | Solve queries against a consistent sample dataset and compare your approach. | Free exercises |
| [The Art of PostgreSQL](https://theartofpostgresql.com/) | Dimitri Fontaine; deepen SQL reasoning with a PostgreSQL-focused book. | Paid book; author samples |
| [High Performance MySQL, Fourth Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/high-performance-mysql/9781492080503/) | Silvia Botros and Jeremy Tinley; study operating and performance trade-offs. | Paid book or subscription |
| [MongoDB: The Definitive Guide, Third Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/mongodb-the-definitive/9781491954454/) | Shannon Bradshaw and coauthors; use the contents to choose a data-modeling or operations chapter. | Paid book or subscription |
| [Dalibo Explain](https://explain.dalibo.com/) | Explore a PostgreSQL plan visually using non-sensitive sample input. | Public plan visualizer; do not upload private plans |
