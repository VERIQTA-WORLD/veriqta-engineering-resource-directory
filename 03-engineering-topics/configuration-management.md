# Configuration Management

Start with a host task you understand manually, then compare ways to express and check it.

## Make a known task repeatable

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ansible for DevOps](https://www.ansiblefordevops.com/) | Jeff Geerling; follow configuration-management examples and compare them with your own host environment. | Paid book; public companion material |
| [Ansible 101 with Jeff Geerling](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2020/ansible-101-jeff-geerling-youtube-streaming-series/) | Watch the episode matching your task; the page provides topic markers and recordings. | Free video series; older examples |
| [UNIX and Linux System Administration Handbook](https://www.admin.com/) | The authors’ site for a broad administration reference; choose relevant chapters and confirm current platform details. | Paid book; author resources |
| [Learn Linux TV](https://www.learnlinux.tv/) | Discover Linux demonstrations and topic series from Jay LaCroix. | Public videos and articles; separate paid products |
