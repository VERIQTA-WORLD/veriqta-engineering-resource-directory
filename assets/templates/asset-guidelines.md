# Visual assets

Original VERIQTA directory artwork uses deep green, mint, and off-white. Covers are 1080 × 1350; SVG sources and PNG exports are included. Arial is preferred; Nimbus Sans is the rendering fallback. Section icons use directory numbers, not third-party trademarks.

`assets/logos/veriqta-wordmark.svg` is a text wordmark; it is not a recreation of the official intertwined V/Q emblem. Third-party logos retain their original artwork. Their provenance is in `assets/logos/sources.json`; their trademark and license terms remain with their owners. Inclusion implies no endorsement.

The screenshots folder is intentionally empty: no website or application has been deployed or captured. The supplied cover template is editable SVG.
