# Cloud Engineering

Choose a provider and follow one workload through identity, networking, compute, and monitoring.

## Choose a starting resource

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AWS Skill Builder](https://skillbuilder.aws/) | Find AWS learning by service or role; inspect the selected course’s lab access. | Free and paid offerings; sign-in varies |
| [Azure training](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/azure/) | Select a module matching the Azure service you are using. | Free reading; exercise accounts and charges vary |
| [Google Cloud training](https://cloud.google.com/learn/training) | Choose a learning path, course, or lab for your current provider task. | Mixed access; account and lab terms vary |
| [AWS Workshops](https://workshops.aws/) | Choose a workshop by service and inspect its setup, account, and teardown instructions. | Free instructions; AWS charges may apply |
| [FinOps Framework](https://www.finops.org/framework/) | Connect cloud cost work with allocation, ownership, and engineering decisions. | Public framework |
