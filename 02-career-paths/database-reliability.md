# Database Reliability Engineering

Discover resources that connect queries and data integrity with operating and recovery decisions.

## Choose a starting resource

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Art of PostgreSQL](https://theartofpostgresql.com/) | Dimitri Fontaine; deepen SQL reasoning with a PostgreSQL-focused book. | Paid book; author samples |
| [High Performance MySQL, Fourth Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/high-performance-mysql/9781492080503/) | Silvia Botros and Jeremy Tinley; study operating and performance trade-offs. | Paid book or subscription |
| [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) | Select a chapter about objectives, alerts, incidents, or releases rather than reading everything in order. | Free online book |
| [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://dataintensive.net/) | Martin Kleppmann’s author site; compare editions and select the data-system question you need to study. | Paid book; author resources |
| [PostgreSQL Exercises](https://pgexercises.com/) | Solve queries against a consistent sample dataset and compare your approach. | Free exercises |
