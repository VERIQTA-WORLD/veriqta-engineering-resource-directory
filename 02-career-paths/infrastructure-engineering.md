# Infrastructure Engineering

Connect compute, networking, storage, and recovery rather than studying each platform in isolation.

## Choose a starting resource

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [UNIX and Linux System Administration Handbook](https://www.admin.com/) | The authors’ site for a broad administration reference; choose relevant chapters and confirm current platform details. | Paid book; author resources |
| [Computer Networks: A Systems Approach](https://book.systemsapproach.org/) | Larry Peterson and Bruce Davie; connect protocols to the systems they support. | Free online textbook |
| [Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/) | Study operating-system concepts with free chapters and associated exercises. | Free online textbook |
| [Terraform: Up & Running](https://www.terraformupandrunning.com/) | Yevgeniy Brikman; connect infrastructure definitions with state, collaboration, and change management. | Paid book; author resources |
| [Learn Linux TV](https://www.learnlinux.tv/) | Discover Linux demonstrations and topic series from Jay LaCroix. | Public videos and articles; separate paid products |
