# Platform Engineering

Discover approaches to giving other engineers a supported way to complete recurring work.

## Choose a starting resource

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CNCF Platforms White Paper](https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/whitepapers/platforms/) | Explore platform capabilities and the needs of the engineers who consume them. | Free white paper |
| [CNCF online programs](https://www.cncf.io/online-programs/) | Browse project and practitioner sessions; choose a recording about your current cloud-native question. | Event catalog; session access varies |
| [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) | Select a chapter about objectives, alerts, incidents, or releases rather than reading everything in order. | Free online book |
| [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://dataintensive.net/) | Martin Kleppmann’s author site; compare editions and select the data-system question you need to study. | Paid book; author resources |
