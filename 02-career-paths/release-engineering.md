# Release Engineering

Study the evidence and controls that move a build into a release you can identify and recover from.

## Choose a starting resource

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [DORA: Continuous delivery](https://dora.dev/capabilities/continuous-delivery/) | Connect delivery tooling to the practices and feedback it is meant to support. | Free engineering guide |
| [Pro Git](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2) | Scott Chacon and Ben Straub; read the chapter matching the Git operation you need to understand. | Free online book |
| [SLSA specification](https://slsa.dev/spec/) | Choose the specification version and investigate build and provenance requirements. | Free specification |
| [Terraform: Up & Running](https://www.terraformupandrunning.com/) | Yevgeniy Brikman; connect infrastructure definitions with state, collaboration, and change management. | Paid book; author resources |
