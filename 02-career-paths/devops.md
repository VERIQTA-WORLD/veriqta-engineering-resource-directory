# DevOps Engineering

Follow a change from source to a working service, and discover resources for the gaps along that path.

## Choose a starting resource

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Pro Git](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2) | Scott Chacon and Ben Straub; read the chapter matching the Git operation you need to understand. | Free online book |
| [DORA: Continuous delivery](https://dora.dev/capabilities/continuous-delivery/) | Connect delivery tooling to the practices and feedback it is meant to support. | Free engineering guide |
| [TechWorld with Nana](https://www.techworld-with-nana.com/) | Discover the creator’s teaching and public video channel; distinguish free videos from paid programs. | Creator catalog; mixed access |
| [Learn Docker in a Month of Lunches](https://www.manning.com/books/learn-docker-in-a-month-of-lunches) | Elton Stoneman; follow a progressive application-oriented route through container use. | Paid book; publisher samples |
| [Ansible 101 with Jeff Geerling](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2020/ansible-101-jeff-geerling-youtube-streaming-series/) | Watch the episode matching your task; the page provides topic markers and recordings. | Free video series; older examples |
