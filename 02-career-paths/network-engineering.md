# Network Engineering

Discover explanations, captures, and labs that help you follow traffic through a network.

## Choose a starting resource

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Networking Fundamentals](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/index/networking-fundamentals-how-data-moves-through-the-internet/) | Follow the free introductory module on how data moves through networks. | Free module; further training separate |
| [Computer Networks: A Systems Approach](https://book.systemsapproach.org/) | Larry Peterson and Bruce Davie; connect protocols to the systems they support. | Free online textbook |
| [Practical OSPF: free first module](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/stand-alone/ospf-training-course-free-m1/) | Study the routing-protocol introduction before an OSPF lab. | Free module; full course separate |
| [SharkFest retrospective](https://sharkfest.wireshark.org/retrospective/) | Explore packet-analysis talks, slides, and specialist sessions. | Archive; recording access varies |
| [containerlab](https://containerlab.dev/) | Build reproducible network labs from topology definitions. | Free documentation and source; compatible host and images required |
| [Stanford CS144](https://cs144.github.io/) | Study computer networking through a university course and programming assignments. | Public material; C++ and systems prerequisites |
