# Observability Engineering

Discover ways to make a service question answerable through useful evidence.

## Choose a starting resource

| Open | What you can find | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OpenTelemetry Demo](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/demo/) | Explore an instrumented application and its signals. | Free source; local or hosted infrastructure required |
| [PromQL cheat sheet](https://promlabs.com/promql-cheat-sheet/) | Choose an expression pattern and adapt it to a metric whose labels you understand. | Free reference; separate training is paid |
| [Prometheus: Up & Running, Second Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/prometheus-up/9781098131135/) | Julien Pivotto and Brian Brazil; choose chapters on collection, queries, and alerting. | Paid book or subscription |
| [Grafana Play](https://play.grafana.org/) | Explore demonstration dashboards without connecting a production data source. | Public demo; available features vary |
| [Systems Performance](https://www.brendangregg.com/systems-performance-2nd-edition-book.html) | Brendan Gregg; study how to frame and investigate performance questions across a system. | Paid book; author material |

## A useful result to work toward

Follow one request and show how its trace, metrics, and logs support an investigation without claiming more than they establish. Use the linked exercise’s setup and access conditions, work with synthetic data, and include cleanup with your notes.
